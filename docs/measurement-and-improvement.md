# Measure outcomes and improve the process

Start with qualified leads, sales, and the reliability of the work that produced them. Keep search and AI observations as supporting evidence. An AI mention, a visit, a conversion event, a qualified lead, and a sale are different measurements.

## Keep four scoreboards

| Scoreboard | Record | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business | Qualified leads, accepted opportunities, sales, collected revenue, and acquisition cost when available | Use an agreed definition and an authenticated business record. State the attribution method. |
| Discovery | Search clicks and impressions, page-level queries, controlled AI samples, and attributable referrals | Report the coverage and conditions. A mention does not establish revenue. |
| Execution | Runs with complete records, verification, successful handoffs, cycle time, and exceptions | Keep partial and failed runs in the denominator where applicable. |
| Public usefulness | Accessible guides, working links, template adoption, and independent continuation tests | A published file is not proof that a reader or agent can use it successfully. |

Do not invent zeros for missing measurements. Report `unavailable`, the reason, and the next collection step.

## Establish a search baseline

Use authenticated Search Console and analytics data when available. Record the property, date window, filters, page or query set, and collection limits privately. Fix country, language, device, location, and checked depth for rank samples. A URL not found within the checked depth has a null position and a coverage note.

Google reports AI Overviews and AI Mode traffic within overall Web search performance. Do not label ordinary Search Console totals as isolated AI traffic. Track landing pages and qualified conversions alongside search data. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

## Establish an AI answer baseline

Create a fixed suite of buyer questions covering your category, your process, and the purchase decision. Test branded and unbranded questions separately. Keep the same suite for comparisons and version substantive changes.

For every sample record:

- Exact question, test date, provider, visible model or product version, locale, and location.
- Whether web search was enabled, account or personalization conditions, and repetition count.
- The response or retained evidence, named businesses, and exact cited URLs.
- Mention present, citation present, and recommendation position as separate fields.
- Collection result: successful, partial, or failed, with the reason.

Use a fresh conversation for each independent question. A practical starter design is three repetitions per question and provider, with the same conditions each week. That is an operating choice, not a platform requirement or proof of statistical significance. Report the sample denominator and variation.

Do not report a provider you could not query. A brand mention without a link is not a citation. A controlled prompt response is a sample, not an account-wide visibility percentage or a guarantee of what every user sees.

## Check accessibility without confusing search and training

For Google AI search, the page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. Maintain crawlable text, internal links, and accurate structured data. Google does not require a special AI file or special schema. [Google's AI feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

For ChatGPT search, review `OAI-SearchBot` access and the hosting or firewall layer. `GPTBot` concerns potential training use and has independent controls. User-requested `ChatGPT-User` retrieval has a separate role. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)

For Claude search, review `Claude-SearchBot`; `Claude-User` handles user-requested retrieval, and `ClaudeBot` concerns potential training use. These roles have separate access controls. [Anthropic crawler documentation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler)

Allowed crawling establishes eligibility to retrieve content. It does not establish ranking, recommendation, or a citation. Check actual responses and citations separately. Treat a failed fetch from an audit tool as a coverage gap until the cause is established.

## Keep advertising evidence tied to the account

Use authenticated advertising data and verified conversion paths for campaign decisions. Check that a tracked event represents the agreed lead or sale. Public ad libraries can establish that a creative was observed under stated conditions. They cannot establish spend, targeting, conversions, profitability, or a competitor's full campaign coverage.

## Run one bounded improvement at a time

1. Select a repeated friction or business gap supported by run evidence.
2. State the hypothesis, affected procedure, owner, expected benefit, and possible regression.
3. Set the acceptance check before making the change.
4. Change the smallest relevant instruction, tool step, or content section.
5. Re-run the original task or a representative test from a fresh session.
6. Have another reviewer inspect the result and evidence.
7. Promote the change only when the acceptance check passes. Otherwise revise or revert the specific change.
8. Link the decision to the source runs and next review date.

Keep public publication, customer communications, account permissions, and spending within the authority granted by the human owner. A process improvement does not expand its own authority.

Bound each weekly cycle to 30 minutes, at most three reversible improvements, and two review iterations. Reuse the same weekly report on retries. Stop the cycle when evidence is insufficient, authority is unavailable, or a test fails. Keep unfinished items visible for the next cycle.

## Weekly update format

Report the date window, completed outcomes with evidence, business and discovery measurements with coverage, failed or partial runs, unresolved blockers, the improvement tested, and the next three priorities. Name decisions that require the human owner. Avoid repeating unchanged observations as new findings.

If no comparable baseline exists, call the current run a baseline. If a collection fails, preserve the failure. The record should make the next action easier to choose.
